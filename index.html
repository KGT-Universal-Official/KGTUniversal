⚡ About The Founder

Hello! I am Krishna Gopal Tak, an Indian developer and the Founder of KGT Universal. I am from Ratangarh in the Churu district of Rajasthan. My primary goal is to craft engaging games and apps designed purely for your entertainment. Operating as a solo developer, I handle every aspect of design and code entirely on my own. While this means I currently focus on indie projects rather than high-end, resource-heavy blockbusters, my passion drives me forward. Moving ahead, I am fully committed to pushing my limits and building deeply immersive, highly competitive experiences with top-tier quality for all of you.
